How to Review Occupations

You are reviewing local occupation titles that need to be matched to the ESCO (European Skills, Competences, Qualifications and Occupations) taxonomy.

The Review Screen

  • Left Panel: Shows the local occupation to review, any suggested match, and your selection
  • Middle Panel: ESCO taxonomy browser - search or navigate the tree to find matches
  • Right Panel: Details about your selected ESCO occupation

Decision Buttons

  • Approve Accept the suggested ESCO match as-is
  • Match Use your own selection from the ESCO tree/search
  • New Local This occupation has no ESCO equivalent - mark as country-specific
  • Skip Unsure - leave for another reviewer

Tips

  • Click the ? next to the local occupation to Google its meaning
  • Use the search box to quickly find ESCO occupations by keyword
  • Double-click a tree item to select it, single-click to expand/collapse
  • Your recently reviewed items appear below - click to re-edit if you made a mistake
